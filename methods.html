Scientific Rigor

Methodology & Clinical Framework

StimBank standardizes human intracranial electrical brain stimulation (EBS) data across neurosurgical modalities, anatomical references, and behavioral observations.

Stereotaxic Coordinate Normalization

Every stimulation contact site archived in StimBank is mapped to standard stereotaxic coordinate space (Montreal Neurological Institute, MNI152 nonlinear 6th-generation asymmetric template).

X-Axis
Left (−) to Right (+)
−78 mm to +78 mm
Y-Axis
Posterior (−) to Anterior (+)
−112 mm to +72 mm
Z-Axis
Inferior (−) to Superior (+)
−50 mm to +84 mm

Coordinates published in Talairach stereotaxic space are converted using the Lancaster transformation (Lancaster et al., 2007) to ensure rigorous spatial congruence.

Intracranial Electrical Stimulation Modalities

Stereo-EEG (sEEG)

Multicontact intracerebral depth electrodes (0.8 mm diameter with 5–18 platinum contacts) implanted stereotaxically. Bipolar stimulation is applied between adjacent contact pairs (50 Hz, 0.5 ms, 0.5–4.0 mA) to map functional eloquence.

Subdural ECoG

Silastic grids and strips placed directly on the cortical pial surface after craniotomy. Electrodes (2.3 mm exposed surface, 10 mm spacing) provide high spatial resolution extraoperative functional mapping over lateral cortex.

Intraoperative DES

Direct electrical stimulation during awake craniotomy for brain tumor (e.g. glioma) resection. A bipolar probe (5 mm spacing) delivers biphasic square pulses (60 Hz, 1 ms, 1.5–5.0 mA) to map safe functional boundaries.

Deep Brain Stimulation

Chronic high-frequency electrical pulses (130–180 Hz, 60–90 μs, 1.5–4.5 mA) delivered through permanently implanted leads targeted at subcortical nuclei (STN, VIM, GPi, Area 25).

The 8 Consensus Categories of Phenomena

Consensus Taxonomy

Evoked behavioral, sensory, cognitive, and subjective responses are organized into 8 standardized neurofunctional domains, each identified by a consistent color throughout the StimBank platform:

Category Definition & Scope Key Anatomical Hubs Included Sub-Domains
Sensory Somatosensory, visual, auditory, gustatory, olfactory, vestibular, and body perception pathways evoking elementary or complex sensations, illusions, and altered body schema. Postcentral Gyrus (S1), Operculum (S2), Calcarine Sulcus (V1), FFA, Heschl's Gyrus (A1), Posterior Insula, TPJ Auditory, Visual, Gustatory & Olfactory, Vestibular, Body Perception, Somatosensory
Motor Primary, supplementary, and subcortical motor circuits controlling voluntary muscle contractions, eye movements, tremor regulation, and motor inhibition. Precentral Gyrus (Hand Knob), SMA, Frontal Eye Field (FEF), Subthalamic Nucleus (STN), VIM Thalamus Upper Limb & Hand, Saccadic Eye, Face & Vocal, Motor Arrest, Tremor Control
Cognitive Higher-order associative networks including language processing, speech arrest, naming anomia, experiential memory retrieval, and Theory of Mind. Pars Opercularis (Broca), Posterior STG (Wernicke), Hippocampus (CA1), Entorhinal Cortex, DLPFC, TPJ Speech Arrest, Language, Déjà vu & Memory, Mentalizing (ToM), Working Memory
Affective Limbic and paralimbic circuits generating subjective emotional states, fear, euphoria, determination, or acute relief of dysphoria. Basolateral Amygdala, Anterior Cingulate (ACC), aMCC, Subgenual Area 25, Nucleus Accumbens Fear & Anxiety, Mirth & Laughter, Perseverance, Mood Lifting, Ecstatic Bliss
Autonomic Central autonomic network regulating visceral interoception, cardiac rhythm, vasomotor tone, and neurovegetative reflexes. Anterior Insular Cortex, Periaqueductal Gray (PAG), Hypothalamus, Opercular Visceral Area Visceral / Aura, Cardiac Dynamics, Vasomotor Tone, Piloerection, Pupillary Reflex
Agency Parietal, insular, and premotor networks governing the sense of bodily ownership, self-location, motor intentionality, and agency of action. Right Angular Gyrus, Right TPJ, Inferior Parietal Lobule (IPL), Premotor Cortex, Pre-SMA Out-of-Body, Motor Intention, Illusory Move, Foreign Limb, Automatisms
Consciousness Frontoparietal, claustrum, intralaminar thalamic, and default mode networks supporting subjective awareness, contact, and global integration. Claustrum / External Capsule, Intralaminar Thalamic Nuclei, Precuneus, Centromedian Thalamus Altered Awareness, Conscious Pause, Absence State, Derealization
No Effect Asymptomatic stimulation contacts where electrical delivery within safety limits evoked no detectable behavioral, cognitive, sensorimotor, or autonomic response. Silent Association Neocortex, Non-Eloquent DLPFC, Subthreshold Frontal White Matter Asymptomatic, Subthreshold, Negative Margin

Pathology & Surgical Context Coverage

Combining data across surgical contexts provides comprehensive causal mapping of both cortical surfaces and deep subcortical structures:

Refractory Epilepsy Monitoring (sEEG & ECoG): Deep insular, amygdalar, hippocampal, and cingulate stereotaxic mapping during pre-surgical evaluation.
Awake Brain Tumor Resection (Intraoperative DES): Eloquent subcortical white matter pathway mapping and functional cortical boundaries across motor, speech, and associative networks.
